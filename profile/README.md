**A vibe graphing IDE.** Don't prompt the result. Build the team. Prompt once.

antiloki puts your AI agents side by side, lets them work together under limits you approve, and draws a team of them as a graph that it runs, tests and improves by experiment. It works with the AI subscriptions you already pay for, through their own CLIs. **Free and open source (Apache-2.0).** Linux and Windows. No account.
